## Test coverage gaps
- **MEDIUM — No regression test locking in the removed auto-save.** `recommend()`
  no longer writes to the playlist, but no test asserts this on the happy path.
  The success-path test (`RecommendationServiceTest` ~line 148) stubs track
  resolution but does not `verify(spotifyApi, never()).addTracksToPlaylist(...)`.
  A future re-introduction of auto-save into `recommend()` — the exact regression
  fixed here — would not be caught by the suite. Recommend adding a
  `verify(..., never()).addTracksToPlaylist(...)` assertion to the happy-path
  `recommend()` test. (The `saveToPlaylist(...)` method itself remains directly
  and adequately unit-tested.)
- **LOW — SecurityConfig change untested.** No test asserts `/mcp/**` requires
  authentication or that `/h2-console/**` is no longer permitted. Risk is low
  because the removed matchers targeted a never-served path, but an explicit
  "unauthenticated `/mcp/**` is rejected" test would guard the exemption boundary.
- **LOW — System prompt presence untested.** Neither `PlaceholderServiceTest` nor
  `RecommendationServiceTest` captures the `MessageCreateParams` to assert a
  system prompt is set. Since CLAUDE.md treats a missing system prompt as a HIGH
  finding, an argument-captor assertion would turn that principle into an
  enforced, regression-safe check rather than relying on review.
